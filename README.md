Welcome to my simple Pokemon Shiny Hunting Tracker!
This app lets you input the name of the game you're hunting in, the Pokemon you're hunting and also let's you adjust any shiny boosting filters you might have.
It will display the sprite of the shiny you're hunting from that game, alongside the odds of catching it, and your previously caught shinies!
Just press either the "+1" button on the app or the "=" on your keyboard to increase the encounter number, and hit the caught button to register the shiny as caught!
